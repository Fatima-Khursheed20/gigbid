# Defect detection on a packaging line

*Client:* PackRight Industries · *Industry:* manufacturing · *Delivered:* 2023-11

## The problem
Mislabelled boxes reached customers despite manual checks.

## What I built
Trained a real-time detector on 6k annotated frames and deployed it on an edge box next to the conveyor. Under the hood this involved containerised deployments.

## Results
Recall 0.94 / precision 0.91 on defects at 28 FPS; customer complaints about labels fell by half.

## Stack
YOLO, PyTorch, OpenCV and a few internal libraries.
