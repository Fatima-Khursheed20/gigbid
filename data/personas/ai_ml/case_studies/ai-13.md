# Resume-to-job matching engine

*Client:* TalentBridge · *Industry:* hr · *Delivered:* 2025-04

## The problem
Recruiters manually screened hundreds of CVs per role.

## What I built
Structured extraction of CVs into skills and experience, then hybrid ranking with explanations. Under the hood this involved serving models behind a typed REST API, LLM application orchestration.

## Results
Recruiter shortlist agreement 0.81 (Cohen's kappa 0.62) on 50 roles.

## Stack
pgvector, Python, Prompt engineering and a few internal libraries.
