# Semantic product search

*Client:* Kitchenly · *Industry:* ecommerce · *Delivered:* 2024-05

## The problem
Customers searching 'something to keep coffee hot' found nothing.

## What I built
Embedded 80k products with a sentence-transformer and served vector search with keyword fallback. Under the hood this involved fine-tuning pretrained transformer models, serving models behind a typed REST API.

## Results
Search-to-cart conversion up 11% in an A/B test; zero-result searches down 64%.

## Stack
Pinecone, Python and a few internal libraries.
