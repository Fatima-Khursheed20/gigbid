# LLM guardrails and red-teaming

*Client:* Tickety · *Industry:* saas · *Delivered:* 2025-10

## The problem
A customer-facing bot could be tricked into revealing internal notes.

## What I built
Built an injection test suite (180 attacks), delimiting and output filters, and a regression gate in CI. Under the hood this involved stateful multi-step LLM agents, strong Python engineering.

## Results
Attack success fell from 34% to 3% on the suite; no regressions in answer quality.

## Stack
Prompt engineering, LLM evaluation.
