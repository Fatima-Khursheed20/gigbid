# Multi-step support agent with tool calls

*Client:* Tickety · *Industry:* saas · *Delivered:* 2025-07

## The problem
Tier-1 support tickets piled up overnight.

## What I built
Designed a stateful agent that classifies tickets, looks up orders and drafts replies for human approval. Under the hood this involved serving models behind a typed REST API, background task queues with retries, designing robust prompts and structured outputs.

## Results
Auto-drafted 61% of tickets; agents accepted 78% of drafts with minor edits.

## Stack
LangGraph, Python, LLM evaluation (plus the usual supporting tools).
