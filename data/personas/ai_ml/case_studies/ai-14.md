# Image classification for crop disease

*Client:* AgriAssist NGO · *Industry:* agriculture · *Delivered:* 2023-04

## The problem
Farmers had no quick way to identify leaf diseases.

## What I built
Transfer-learned a small CNN and shipped it as an offline-capable API for a mobile app. Under the hood this involved serving models behind a typed REST API.

## Results
Top-1 accuracy 93% across 12 diseases; used by ~2,000 farmers in the pilot.

## Stack
PyTorch, OpenCV and a few internal libraries.
