# Contract Q&A assistant for a legal-tech startup

*Client:* Clausewise Ltd · *Industry:* legal · *Delivered:* 2025-03

## The problem
Paralegals spent hours searching 40k contracts for indemnity and termination clauses.

## What I built
Built a retrieval pipeline with clause-aware chunking, hybrid search and citations, served behind an API. Under the hood this involved vector search inside Postgres, designing robust prompts and structured outputs.

## Results
Cut clause lookup from ~25 minutes to under 1 minute; 92% of answers cited the correct clause in a 200-question audit.

## Stack
Python, RAG, LangChain, FastAPI (plus the usual supporting tools).
