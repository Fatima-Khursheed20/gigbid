# Model monitoring and retraining service

*Client:* LendFast · *Industry:* fintech · *Delivered:* 2025-01

## The problem
A credit model silently degraded for months.

## What I built
Set up drift detection, scheduled retraining and a model registry with approval gates. Under the hood this involved scheduled data pipelines (DAGs), strong Python engineering.

## Results
Drift is caught within 24h; retraining went from a 2-week manual task to a 40-minute pipeline.

## Stack
MLflow, AWS SageMaker, Docker.
