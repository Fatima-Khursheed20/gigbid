# Churn prediction for a telecom

*Client:* Connectly · *Industry:* telecom · *Delivered:* 2023-06

## The problem
Retention offers were sent to everyone, wasting budget.

## What I built
Built a calibrated churn classifier with SHAP explanations and tracked experiments. Under the hood this involved experiment tracking and model registry, data wrangling in pandas.

## Results
AUC 0.87; targeting the top decile saved ~$40k per quarter in offer costs.

## Stack
scikit-learn, XGBoost, Python.
