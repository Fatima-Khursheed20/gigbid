# Async document processing backend

*Client:* Clausewise Ltd · *Industry:* legal · *Delivered:* 2025-06

## The problem
Uploading a 300-page contract timed out the web app.

## What I built
Moved parsing and embedding into a queue of background workers with retries and progress events. Under the hood this involved background task queues with retries, caching and pub/sub, containerised deployments.

## Results
Upload timeouts went to zero; throughput 1,200 pages/minute on two workers.

## Stack
Python, FastAPI and a few internal libraries.
