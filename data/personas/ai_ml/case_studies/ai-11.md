# Research-paper RAG over arXiv

*Client:* personal open-source · *Industry:* education · *Delivered:* 2025-09

## The problem
Students struggled to find relevant prior work quickly.

## What I built
Open-source tool with section-aware chunking, reranking and answer-faithfulness checks. Under the hood this involved LLM application orchestration, strong Python engineering.

## Results
340 GitHub stars; faithfulness 0.89 on a 150-question benchmark I released.

## Stack
RAG, FAISS, LLM evaluation.
