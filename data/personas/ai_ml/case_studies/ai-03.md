# Retail demand forecasting

*Client:* Bazaar Mart · *Industry:* retail · *Delivered:* 2024-02

## The problem
Store managers over-ordered perishables before holidays.

## What I built
Combined a seasonal forecaster with a gradient-boosted residual model, refreshed nightly. Under the hood this involved data wrangling in pandas, scheduled data pipelines (DAGs).

## Results
MAPE dropped from 31% to 14% across 120 SKUs; waste fell ~18% in the pilot stores.

## Stack
Prophet, Python, XGBoost.
