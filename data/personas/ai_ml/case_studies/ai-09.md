# Medical-notes de-identification

*Client:* CarePath Clinics · *Industry:* healthcare · *Delivered:* 2024-12

## The problem
Researchers could not use clinical notes until PHI was removed.

## What I built
Combined rule patterns with a fine-tuned NER model to redact names, dates and IDs. Under the hood this involved strong Python engineering, systematic evaluation of LLM outputs.

## Results
PHI recall 0.98 on 1,000 annotated notes, audited by the clinic's privacy officer.

## Stack
spaCy, Hugging Face Transformers and a few internal libraries.
