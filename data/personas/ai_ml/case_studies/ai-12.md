# Real-time call-centre sentiment

*Client:* VoiceHub · *Industry:* telecom · *Delivered:* 2024-03

## The problem
Supervisors only learned about angry callers after the call ended.

## What I built
Streaming transcription chunks scored for sentiment and pushed to a live supervisor view. Under the hood this involved caching and pub/sub, containerised deployments.

## Results
Median alert latency 1.8 s; escalations resolved 22% faster.

## Stack
Hugging Face Transformers, FastAPI and a few internal libraries.
