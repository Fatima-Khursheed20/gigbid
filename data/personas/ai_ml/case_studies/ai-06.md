# Brand-voice copy model

*Client:* Loom & Line Agency · *Industry:* marketing · *Delivered:* 2025-05

## The problem
Copywriters rewrote generic LLM drafts to match each client's tone.

## What I built
Fine-tuned a 7B open model with adapters per brand and built a blind tone-match evaluation. Under the hood this involved training deep neural networks, systematic evaluation of LLM outputs.

## Results
Editors preferred the tuned model's drafts 71% of the time in a blind test of 300 pairs.

## Stack
Hugging Face Transformers, LoRA fine-tuning and a few internal libraries.
