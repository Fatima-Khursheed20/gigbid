# Invoice data extraction pipeline

*Client:* PayLedger · *Industry:* fintech · *Delivered:* 2024-09

## The problem
An accounts team keyed in 3,000 scanned invoices a month by hand.

## What I built
OCR with image deskewing, then entity extraction for vendor, totals, dates and tax IDs. Under the hood this involved classical image processing, serving models behind a typed REST API.

## Results
Field-level accuracy 96% on a held-out set of 500 invoices; manual entry time down 70%.

## Stack
Tesseract OCR, spaCy, Python and a few internal libraries.
