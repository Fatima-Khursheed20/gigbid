# Pricing A/B test analysis

*Client:* TenantKit Cloud · *Industry:* saas · *Delivered:* 2025-05

## The problem
A startup wanted to raise prices without losing sign-ups.

## What I built
Designed a 3-arm experiment with power analysis and sequential monitoring. Under the hood this involved strong Python engineering, complex analytical SQL (window functions, CTEs).

## Results
Shipped the +15% arm: revenue per visitor +9% with no significant drop in conversion (p=0.41).

## Stack
A/B testing, Statistics.
