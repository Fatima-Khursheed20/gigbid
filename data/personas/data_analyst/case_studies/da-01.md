# Executive sales dashboard

*Client:* Bazaar Mart · *Industry:* retail · *Delivered:* 2024-04

## The problem
Leadership got sales numbers two weeks late in emailed spreadsheets.

## What I built
Star-schema model, DAX measures for YoY and margin, and row-level security per region. Under the hood this involved advanced Excel modelling (pivots, Power Query), extract-transform-load pipelines.

## Results
Daily refresh; the weekly reporting meeting was cut from 2 hours to 30 minutes.

## Stack
Power BI, SQL.
