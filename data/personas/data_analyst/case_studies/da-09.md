# Hospital operations dashboard

*Client:* CarePath Clinics · *Industry:* healthcare · *Delivered:* 2024-02

## The problem
Bed occupancy was tracked on a whiteboard.

## What I built
Live occupancy, length-of-stay and discharge forecasts for 6 wards. Under the hood this involved extract-transform-load pipelines.

## Results
Average ER boarding time down 18%.

## Stack
Tableau, SQL.
