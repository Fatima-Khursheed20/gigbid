# Data cleaning and ETL for a CRM merge

*Client:* Skyline Realtors · *Industry:* real-estate · *Delivered:* 2024-05

## The problem
Two merged agencies had 60k duplicated, inconsistent contact records.

## What I built
Fuzzy de-duplication, standardisation rules and a reviewed merge file. Under the hood this involved strong Python engineering, complex analytical SQL (window functions, CTEs).

## Results
60k records reduced to 38k clean contacts with a 99% match-review accuracy.

## Stack
ETL, pandas, Excel and a few internal libraries.
