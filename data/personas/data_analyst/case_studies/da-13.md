# Interactive Streamlit KPI explorer

*Client:* LendFast · *Industry:* fintech · *Delivered:* 2025-10

## The problem
Credit risk team wanted to slice approval rates without writing SQL.

## What I built
Internal web app with filters, cohort charts and CSV export on top of the warehouse. Under the hood this involved complex analytical SQL (window functions, CTEs), clear charts with matplotlib/seaborn/Plotly.

## Results
Used daily by 14 analysts; replaced 9 static reports.

## Stack
Streamlit, pandas (plus the usual supporting tools).
