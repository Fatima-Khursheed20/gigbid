# Website funnel and GA4 audit

*Client:* Kitchenly · *Industry:* ecommerce · *Delivered:* 2024-07

## The problem
An online shop saw traffic grow but sales stay flat.

## What I built
Fixed event tracking, built a funnel report and found a broken mobile checkout step. Under the hood this involved BigQuery analytics, complex analytical SQL (window functions, CTEs).

## Results
Mobile conversion rose from 1.1% to 1.9% after the fix.

## Stack
Google Analytics 4, Looker Studio and a few internal libraries.
