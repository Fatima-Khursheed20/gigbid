# Subscription retention analysis

*Client:* Tickety · *Industry:* saas · *Delivered:* 2024-11

## The problem
A SaaS company did not know why customers cancelled after month three.

## What I built
Cohort retention curves, feature-usage correlation and a churn-driver memo. Under the hood this involved data wrangling in pandas, clear charts with matplotlib/seaborn/Plotly.

## Results
Identified onboarding gap; a fix lifted month-3 retention from 61% to 69%.

## Stack
Cohort analysis, SQL, Python.
