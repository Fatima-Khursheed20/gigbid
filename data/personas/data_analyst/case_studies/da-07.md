# Marketing attribution in BigQuery

*Client:* Loom & Line Agency · *Industry:* marketing · *Delivered:* 2024-09

## The problem
An agency could not tell clients which channels drove sales.

## What I built
Built position-based attribution from raw GA4 export and ad-platform costs. Under the hood this involved GA4 event analysis, clear charts with matplotlib/seaborn/Plotly.

## Results
Reallocated 20% of spend; client ROAS improved from 2.1 to 2.8.

## Stack
BigQuery, SQL.
