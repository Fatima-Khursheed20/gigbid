# Survey statistics report in R

*Client:* LearnBridge · *Industry:* education · *Delivered:* 2023-08

## The problem
A school network needed a rigorous analysis of a 4,000-respondent parent survey.

## What I built
Weighted estimates, regression on satisfaction drivers and a plain-language report. Under the hood this involved clear charts with matplotlib/seaborn/Plotly.

## Results
Board adopted 3 of 4 recommendations; report cited in the annual plan.

## Stack
R, Statistics and a few internal libraries.
