# Customer segmentation for loyalty

*Client:* Bazaar Mart · *Industry:* retail · *Delivered:* 2025-03

## The problem
One-size-fits-all loyalty emails got poor engagement.

## What I built
RFM features and clustering into 6 segments with personas and playbooks. Under the hood this involved data wrangling in pandas, classical ML pipelines with cross-validation, Tableau visual analytics.

## Results
Email click-through up 43% after segment-specific campaigns.

## Stack
Customer segmentation, Python and a few internal libraries.
