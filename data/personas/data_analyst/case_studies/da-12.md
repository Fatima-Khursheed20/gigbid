# Self-serve analytics with Metabase

*Client:* TenantKit Cloud · *Industry:* saas · *Delivered:* 2025-08

## The problem

## What I built
Curated models, a metrics glossary and training for 20 PMs. Under the hood this involved relational schema design and SQL, containerised deployments.

## Results
Ad-hoc analyst requests down 50% in two months.

## Stack
Metabase, SQL and a few internal libraries.
