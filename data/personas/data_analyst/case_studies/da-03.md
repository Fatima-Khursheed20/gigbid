# Modern data stack migration

*Client:* PayLedger · *Industry:* fintech · *Delivered:* 2025-02

## The problem
Metrics differed between teams because each had its own spreadsheets.

## What I built
Moved reporting to a warehouse with tested, documented models and scheduled runs. Under the hood this involved scheduled data pipelines (DAGs), complex analytical SQL (window functions, CTEs).

## Results
48 dbt models with 160 tests; 'which number is right' tickets dropped to near zero.

## Stack
dbt, Snowflake.
