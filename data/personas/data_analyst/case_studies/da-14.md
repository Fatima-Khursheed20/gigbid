# Revenue forecasting for a marketplace

*Client:* Craftly (startup) · *Industry:* ecommerce · *Delivered:* 2024-12

## The problem
A marketplace needed GMV projections for a fundraising deck.

## What I built
Built a hierarchical forecast by category with scenario bands. Under the hood this involved regression and hypothesis testing, data wrangling in pandas.

## Results
Actuals landed within the 80% band for 5 of 6 months.

## Stack
Forecasting, Python and a few internal libraries.
