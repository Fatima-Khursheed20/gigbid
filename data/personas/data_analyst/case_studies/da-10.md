# Automated reporting pipeline

*Client:* Rapid Couriers · *Industry:* logistics · *Delivered:* 2025-07

## The problem
An analyst spent every Monday morning copying numbers into slides.

## What I built
Scheduled pipeline loads data, runs checks and refreshes self-serve dashboards. Under the hood this involved complex analytical SQL (window functions, CTEs), data wrangling in pandas, self-serve BI with Metabase.

## Results
Saved ~6 hours per week; zero missed reports in 6 months.

## Stack
Airflow, Python and a few internal libraries.
