# Inventory demand forecast in Excel

*Client:* Corner Pharmacy Chain · *Industry:* retail · *Delivered:* 2023-10

## The problem
Pharmacies ran out of seasonal medicines every winter.

## What I built
Seasonal forecasting model in Excel with Power Query refresh, usable by non-technical staff. Under the hood this involved regression and hypothesis testing.

## Results
Stock-outs during peak season down 35%.

## Stack
Excel, Forecasting (plus the usual supporting tools).
