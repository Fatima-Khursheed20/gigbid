# Background export service for a B2B portal

*Client:* Rapid Couriers · *Industry:* logistics · *Delivered:* 2025-10

## The problem
Customers' monthly CSV exports timed out and support had to email files manually.

## What I built
Moved exports to queued background jobs with progress notifications and signed download links. Under the hood this involved Node job queues, caching and pub/sub, serverless functions.

## Results
Export failures went from 15% to under 0.5%; support tickets about exports stopped.

## Stack
Node.js, PostgreSQL (plus the usual supporting tools).
