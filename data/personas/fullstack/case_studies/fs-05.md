# Shopify inventory sync app

*Client:* Kitchenly · *Industry:* ecommerce · *Delivered:* 2024-06

## The problem
Overselling happened because a warehouse system and Shopify disagreed.

## What I built
Custom app that reconciles stock every minute with a queue and conflict rules. Under the hood this involved Node job queues, relational schema design and SQL.

## Results
Overselling incidents went from ~40/month to 2/month.

## Stack
Shopify, Node.js.
