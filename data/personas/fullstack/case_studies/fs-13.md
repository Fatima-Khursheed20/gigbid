# Event ticketing with QR check-in

*Client:* FestHub · *Industry:* events · *Delivered:* 2024-03

## The problem
Paper tickets caused long queues and fraud at festivals.

## What I built
Ticket sales, signed QR codes and an offline-tolerant scanner app. Under the hood this involved component-based single-page frontends, real-time bidirectional updates.

## Results
Scanned 9,000 attendees in 90 minutes; zero duplicate entries.

## Stack
Express, MongoDB, Stripe (plus the usual supporting tools).
