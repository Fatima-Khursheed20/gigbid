# Serverless image pipeline

*Client:* Snapfolio · *Industry:* media · *Delivered:* 2023-12

## The problem
Photographers waited minutes for previews to appear.

## What I built
Event-driven resizing and watermarking on upload with CDN invalidation. Under the hood this involved containerised deployments, automated build and deploy pipelines.

## Results
Preview latency under 2 s at p95; infra bill $11/month for 200k images.

## Stack
AWS Lambda, Node.js (plus the usual supporting tools).
