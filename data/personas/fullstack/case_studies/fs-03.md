# Marketplace MVP for handmade goods

*Client:* Craftly (startup) · *Industry:* ecommerce · *Delivered:* 2023-09

## The problem
Founders needed a working two-sided marketplace for a pilot in six weeks.

## What I built
Seller onboarding, listings, cart, split payouts and Google sign-in. Under the hood this involved utility-first styling, relational schema design and SQL, social login and OAuth flows.

## Results
Launched in 5.5 weeks; 120 sellers and $18k GMV in the first month.

## Stack
Next.js, TypeScript, Stripe (plus the usual supporting tools).
