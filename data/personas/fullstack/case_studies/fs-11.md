# Multi-tenant SaaS starter

*Client:* personal open-source · *Industry:* saas · *Delivered:* 2025-08

## The problem
I kept rebuilding the same auth/billing/tenancy boilerplate for clients.

## What I built
Open-source starter with row-level tenancy, invites, billing and audit logs. Under the hood this involved relational schema design and SQL, utility-first styling, payments and subscriptions integration.

## Results
210 GitHub stars; reused in 4 client projects to save ~3 weeks each.

## Stack
Next.js, Prisma, OAuth.
