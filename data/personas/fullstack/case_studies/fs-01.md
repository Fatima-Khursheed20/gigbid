# Subscription billing for a fitness SaaS

*Client:* PulseGym · *Industry:* fitness · *Delivered:* 2024-08

## The problem
Gym owners collected memberships manually by bank transfer.

## What I built
Built plans, trials, proration and dunning emails with webhooks and an admin dashboard. Under the hood this involved typed ORM migrations, type-safe JavaScript codebases.

## Results
1,400 paying members migrated in two weeks; failed-payment recovery 38%.

## Stack
Stripe, Next.js, PostgreSQL and a few internal libraries.
