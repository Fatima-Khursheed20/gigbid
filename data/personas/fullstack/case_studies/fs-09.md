# Internal CRM on Django

*Client:* Skyline Realtors · *Industry:* real-estate · *Delivered:* 2023-05

## The problem
Agents tracked leads in personal notebooks.

## What I built
Lead pipeline, role-based admin, CSV import and weekly reports. Under the hood this involved containerised deployments, caching and pub/sub.

## Results
Lead response time halved; adopted by all 35 agents.

## Stack
Django, PostgreSQL and a few internal libraries.
