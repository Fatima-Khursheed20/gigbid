# Cross-platform fitness companion app

*Client:* PulseGym · *Industry:* fitness · *Delivered:* 2025-06

## The problem
Members wanted to book classes from their phones.

## What I built
iOS/Android app sharing 90% code, with social login and push notifications. Under the hood this involved JavaScript backend services, social login and OAuth flows.

## Results
4.7-star rating; 60% of bookings moved to mobile in two months.

## Stack
React Native, TypeScript and a few internal libraries.
