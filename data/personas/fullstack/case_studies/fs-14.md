# Headless CMS website with i18n

*Client:* LearnBridge · *Industry:* education · *Delivered:* 2025-09

## The problem
A school network needed one site in English, Urdu and Arabic.

## What I built
Static-first site with RTL support, CMS previews and image optimisation. Under the hood this involved type-safe JavaScript codebases, automated build and deploy pipelines.

## Results
Lighthouse 98; organic traffic up 70% in three months.

## Stack
Next.js, Tailwind CSS and a few internal libraries.
