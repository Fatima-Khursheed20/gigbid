# Clinic appointment booking platform

*Client:* CarePath Clinics · *Industry:* healthcare · *Delivered:* 2025-04

## The problem
Receptionists double-booked doctors using a shared spreadsheet.

## What I built
Booking flows with availability rules, SMS reminders and an audit log. Under the hood this involved type-safe JavaScript codebases, unit testing JavaScript, containerised deployments.

## Results
No double bookings since launch; no-shows down 27% with reminders.

## Stack
React, NestJS, PostgreSQL (plus the usual supporting tools).
