# Real-time logistics tracking dashboard

*Client:* Rapid Couriers · *Industry:* logistics · *Delivered:* 2025-02

## The problem
Dispatchers phoned drivers to ask where they were.

## What I built
Live map with driver positions pushed from a Node service, plus ETA alerts. Under the hood this involved real-time bidirectional updates, caching and pub/sub, type-safe JavaScript codebases.

## Results
Dispatcher calls dropped 60%; 300 concurrent drivers on a $40/month server.

## Stack
React, Node.js and a few internal libraries.
